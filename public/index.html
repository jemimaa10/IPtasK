How it works

Four steps from "I'll do it later" to verified done.

Leader creates the group

Sign up with just a username and password. The group leader creates a group and invites members by username.

Split the jobdesks

The leader assigns a mission to every member — themselves included — each with its own deadline. Daily or weekly targets work too. AI can draft a fair split.

Upload proof of work

Done? Post a screenshot or file with a caption to the Proof feed. Teammates press Checked or send a Revision note.

Miss it? Get exposed.

No proof by the deadline, or a teammate who never checked — IPtasK Bot auto-posts a confession card with their profile photo to the circle.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can create groups and assign missions?

Anyone can create a group and becomes its leader. Only the leader invites members and assigns missions — including one for themselves, to keep the split fair. You can always discuss the split in the group chat first, or let the AI draft it.

What exactly triggers a confession post?

Two things. (1) The deadline passes and the assignee hasn't uploaded any proof. (2) The verification window closes (default 24h after the deadline) and some teammate never pressed Checked or Revision — then that teammate is exposed, and so is the work owner because their work stays unverified. If a reviewer asked for revision and it was never fixed, only the work owner is exposed.

Why is there a verification window after the deadline?

So someone who uploads one minute before the deadline doesn't get their teammates punished for not checking instantly. Leaders can set the window from 1 to 168 hours.

Is a Revision required?

No. Checked is required from every other member; Revision is optional and only used when something needs to be fixed. The revision note is sent to the assignee as an in-app notification.

Can I set daily habits, not just one-off tasks?

Yes. When adding a mission, choose "Every day" or "Every week" and how many times. Each occurrence has its own deadline, proof and checks.

Where are my files stored? Who can see them?

Proof files are only visible to members of that group. Confession cards can be saved as an image if your group wants to share them elsewhere.
